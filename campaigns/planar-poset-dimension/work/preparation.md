Prepared 2026-09-26. The fixed corpus contains 120 distinct 3-SAT formulas: 20 hand cases and 100 seeded random cases, with 64 YES and 56 NO. Formula sizes range from 0 to 6 variables. `generate_cases.py` fixes inputs and seeds before construction.

Run `uv sync --locked` and `uv run --locked python campaigns/planar-poset-dimension/work/check.py --self-test`. It first runs the copied `research/validate_preparation.py`, regenerates random formulas, recomputes Z3 SAT labels, validates assignments directly and compares with exhaustive Boolean assignments. The target promise checker uses exact rational arithmetic for strictly upward polyline segments and pairwise intersection, checks that covers form an acyclic transitive reduction, and rejects edges through unrelated vertices. The target Z3 encoding chooses `d` vertex-order permutations, imposes every comparability in all orders and both orientations of every incomparable pair somewhere. Direct comparison of the order intersection validates each model. Full enumeration of linear extensions and their products agreed on 100 small planar diagrams: 69 YES and 31 NO. Hand cases cover a chain, antichain, bent upward route, crossing routes, transitive non-cover edge and invalid order. An intentionally incorrect candidate returning a singleton poset and always extracting NO-SOLUTION was rejected.

Oracle: CPython 3.12.14 and locked `z3-solver` 4.16.0.0. Only `unsat` yields NO; `unknown` raises. The target crosschecks use at most five elements and dimension budgets one or two. The candidate gate checks at most three target realizers per source case and does not prove universal recovery.
